Can Indians Get an Australian Work Visa Without a Degree?

What the Skills in Demand (482) Visa Really Asks For

📅 November 2026 ⏱ 7 min Read ✍ Global Gateway Internationals

💡 Quick Overview

The short answer is yes, it can be possible. Australia's main employer-sponsored work visa is the Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482). It does not have one general rule that says "you must have a degree".

What matters is a different set of things:

  • Your job must be on the Core Skills Occupation List.
  • You need at least 12 months of relevant work experience.
  • You must meet the qualification, licence or registration rule for that job. For many trade and care jobs, this is a vocational certificate, not a degree.
  • An approved employer must sponsor you and pay at least the minimum salary.

Not every job qualifies. A job title like cleaner, packing helper, warehouse helper or general labourer does not automatically meet the rules.

Note: A$ means Australian dollars. Rupee amounts in brackets are approximate, based on A$1 = ₹67 (late October 2026). The rate changes every day. Australian salary thresholds are yearly amounts, not monthly.

What's Actually Different Here

Many people think "no degree" means "no work visa". It is not that simple. The 482 visa has three streams, and only one of them is the usual route for workers without a degree.

Core Skills stream: This is the main route. Your job must be on the Core Skills Occupation List.

Specialist Skills stream: This is for higher-paid jobs in selected job groups. Trade occupations are excluded, and the salary threshold is much higher.

Labour Agreement stream: This depends on a special agreement that your employer has with the government.

"No degree" does not mean "no proof". You still have to prove your experience, and some jobs need a certificate, a licence or a skills assessment. Also, your employer sponsors you. Nobody can sell you this visa.

How the 482 Visa Works for You

01

Core Skills Stream: The Usual Route Without a Degree

The Core Skills stream covers jobs on the Core Skills Occupation List. This list includes many trade jobs, such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters and welders, and many hospitality jobs, such as chefs and cooks. It also includes care jobs, such as aged care workers and nursing support workers.

Each job on the list has its own rules. For many of these jobs, the usual requirement is a vocational qualification, such as a Certificate III or higher, plus at least 12 months of experience. A degree is not the main test. Check the exact rule for your job code.

The visa lets you work for up to four years. There is no age limit for the 482 visa.

Watch out: being on the list depends on the exact job code, not just the job title. Routine clerical, administrative and entry-level jobs are generally not on the list. Check your job on the official Home Affairs list before you accept an offer.

02

Experience, Skills Assessment and English

You need at least 12 months of experience in the job or a closely related field. This must be within the last 5 years. It does not need to be continuous. Part-time and casual work counts if it adds up to 12 months of full-time work.

For some jobs, a skills assessment is compulsory before you apply. This mostly applies to some trade and cooking jobs, such as electrician, chef, cook, carpenter and welder, for passport holders of certain countries. India is on the list for some of these jobs. The assessment is done by Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) or VETASSESS, depending on the job. There are some exemptions, so check your own case.

For English, tests taken on or after 13 September 2025 need at least IELTS 5.0 in each band, or an equal score in other approved tests. Results must be less than 3 years old, and at-home online tests are not accepted.

Watch out: if your job needs a skills assessment and you do not have one, your application can be invalid. Start the assessment early, because it can take time.

03

Salary and the Sponsoring Employer

From 1 July 2026, the minimum salary for the Core Skills stream is A$79,423 (₹53.2 lakh) a year. It was A$76,515 (₹51.3 lakh) before this date. The Specialist Skills threshold is A$146,576 (₹98.2 lakh) a year.

Your employer must also pay the Annual Market Salary Rate. This is what an Australian worker in the same job and place would earn. The higher of the two amounts applies, so a job at the bare minimum may not be enough.

The employer must be an approved sponsor, and must nominate you for a real job. A recruiter or agent cannot replace the employer's duties. You also need health insurance for your stay, a medical check and police certificates.

Watch out: ask for a written job offer that names the visa subclass 482 and the salary. A tourist visa does not give you permission to work in Australia.

04

From a 482 Work Visa to PR

The 482 visa does not automatically give you permanent residence. Some visa holders can later apply for PR through the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186).

For the Temporary Residence Transition route, the usual rule is about 2 years of full-time sponsored work in the 3 years before you apply, and the employer who most recently sponsored you must nominate you. The age limit is usually under 45, with some exemptions.

If you lose your sponsoring job, you generally have 180 days to find a new approved sponsor or leave Australia.

Watch out: PR rules change often. Check the current 186 rules and your own age and employer position before you plan around PR.

Which Job Fits You?

You are an electrician, welder, carpenter, fitter or mechanic.

→ Trade jobs are often on the list. Check your job code, your certificate and whether a skills assessment is compulsory for your passport.

You are a chef, cook or restaurant or café manager.

→ Hospitality jobs can qualify. Chef and cook jobs may need a skills assessment, so check this first.

You work in aged care or as a nursing support worker.

→ Care jobs can qualify. They often need a vocational certificate, and some roles need registration.

You work as a helper, cleaner, packer or general labourer.

→ These job titles do not automatically qualify. Do not trust anyone who promises a 482 visa for a general helper job.

How to Check Your Eligibility Before You Apply

✔

Find your exact job code (ANZSCO) and check that it is on the Core Skills Occupation List on the Home Affairs website.

✔

Check the qualification, licence or registration rule for that job. Do not assume that no degree means no proof.

✔

Count your experience. You need 12 months in the last 5 years, with payslips, contracts and reference letters as proof.

✔

Check if your job needs a skills assessment for Indian passport holders, and start it early.

✔

Book an approved English test. At-home online tests are not accepted.

✔

Ask the employer if they are an approved sponsor, and get the job offer in writing, with the visa subclass and salary.

✔

Check that the salary meets the threshold and the market salary rate for the job.

✔

Never pay anyone for a job offer or a guaranteed visa. Check that any migration agent is registered.

The Common Mistake People Make

The first mistake is giving up because of the word "degree". Many trade, hospitality and care jobs are judged on experience, certificates and a sponsor, not on a degree.

The second mistake is the opposite. People believe any job title will work if they have no degree. It will not. Your job must be on the list, your experience must be real, and your employer must pay at least the minimum salary.

Bottom Line

An Australian work visa without a degree is possible through the Core Skills stream of the 482 visa, if your job is on the list, you have 12 months of relevant experience, and an approved employer sponsors you at the required salary. Some jobs also need a certificate, a licence or a skills assessment. The visa does not guarantee PR. Check the current rules on the official Home Affairs website, because these figures change every year.

FAQ

Can Indians get an Australian work visa without a degree? ▼

Yes, in many cases. The Skills in Demand (482) visa does not have a general degree rule. Your job must be on the Core Skills Occupation List, you need at least 12 months of relevant experience, and an approved employer must sponsor you. Some jobs need a certificate, licence or skills assessment.

Which visa is used for an employer-sponsored job in Australia? ▼

The Skills in Demand visa, subclass 482. It replaced the Temporary Skill Shortage visa on 7 December 2024. It has three streams: Core Skills, Specialist Skills and Labour Agreement.

How much experience do I need? ▼

At least 12 months in the job or a closely related field, within the last 5 years. It does not need to be continuous, and part-time or casual work counts if it adds up to 12 months of full-time work.

Do I need IELTS for the 482 visa? ▼

Usually yes, unless you are exempt. For tests taken on or after 13 September 2025, you need at least IELTS 5.0 in each band, or an equal score in another approved test. At-home online tests are not accepted.

What is the minimum salary for the 482 visa? ▼

From 1 July 2026, the Core Skills threshold is A$79,423 (₹53.2 lakh) a year, and the Specialist Skills threshold is A$146,576 (₹98.2 lakh) a year. The employer must also meet the market salary rate for the job, and the higher amount applies.

Can I get PR through a 482 visa? ▼

Not automatically. Some 482 holders can later apply for PR through the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186). The usual rule is about 2 years of full-time sponsored work, with the same employer nominating you. Check the current rules.

Can I apply for the 482 visa on my own? ▼

No. Your employer must be an approved sponsor and must nominate you for the job first. Be careful with anyone who asks you for money to "arrange" a job offer or guarantees a visa.
